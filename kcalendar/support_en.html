💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Is an internet connection required for lunar dates and solar terms?

No. All traditional calendar algorithms operate 100% on-device. The app works fully offline (including in Airplane Mode).

Q2. Are my private notes and schedules uploaded to any server?

Never. K-Calendar has no user accounts or remote database. All your data stays locally on your device with complete privacy.

Q3. Is the app completely free?

Yes. K-Calendar is 100% free with no in-app purchases required.
