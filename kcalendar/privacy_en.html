K-Calendar Privacy Policy

K-Calendar prioritizes your personal privacy. We do not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

1. Personal Information Collected

K-Calendar does not require account registration and collects no personally identifiable information (PII).

  • User Data: Schedules, memos, diaries, and birthday inputs are strictly stored on the device's local storage (CoreData / SQLite / Keychain).
  • Calendar Access: If you grant calendar permissions, it is used solely to display local device events and is never transmitted externally.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

K-Calendar uses Google AdMob to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements depending on user consent (App Tracking Transparency).

  • Ad providers may process anonymized device identifiers (IDFA) in compliance with Apple ATT guidelines.
  • Learn more about Google AdMob Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

3. Data Storage & Security

All user entries remain local to the device. Uninstalling the app removes all associated local data unless backed up via iOS device backups.
